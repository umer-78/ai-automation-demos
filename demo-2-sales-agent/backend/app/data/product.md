# Acme Cloud Security: what we sell

Acme Shield watches a company's AWS, Google Cloud and Azure accounts for risky settings, and keeps the
evidence an auditor asks for during SOC 2 and ISO 27001. (Acme Cloud Security is a fictional company
used for this demo.)

## Products and pricing

- **Shield Posture**: continuous cloud configuration checks, 300+ rules, alerts in Slack and Jira.
  From $1,500 a month for up to three cloud accounts.
- **Shield Comply**: SOC 2 and ISO 27001 evidence collection, policy templates, an auditor portal.
  From $1,100 a month.
- **Bundle**: both, from $2,300 a month. Annual contracts, with a 14-day pilot.

## Ideal customer

- B2B SaaS companies with 200 to 2,000 employees, running on AWS or Google Cloud.
- Usually a first or second security hire, or no security team yet: the CTO or VP Engineering owns security.
- Triggers: preparing for SOC 2 or ISO 27001, enterprise customers sending security questionnaires,
  a new funding round, hiring a security engineer, a recent incident at the company or a peer.
- Regions: North America, the UK and the EU.
- Not a fit: security vendors (they build their own tooling), companies under 50 people,
  public-sector buyers with on-premise mandates.

## Why customers pick us

- Setup in a day through read-only cloud roles; no agents to install.
- One place for posture findings and audit evidence, so the first security hire is not running two tools.
- Plain-English findings with the fix, ranked by real exposure.

## Tone for outreach

Short and specific, no fear-mongering. One real observation about them, one clear question.
Address the team, not a named person: the rep adds the contact before sending.
