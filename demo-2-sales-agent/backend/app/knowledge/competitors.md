# Competitors (fictional, for the demo)

## Bastion Cloud

- Enterprise cloud security platform. Strong runtime scanning, large partner network.
- List price starts around $90,000 a year; deployments take 6 to 10 weeks.
- Compliance evidence is an add-on and needs a consultant to map controls.
- **Where we win:** mid-size teams who need SOC 2 or ISO 27001 evidence this quarter and cannot wait for a long rollout.
- **Where we lose:** large enterprises that want one vendor for everything, including on-premise.

## ComplyFlow

- Compliance automation. Good policy templates and auditor marketplace.
- Pulls evidence from HR and ticketing tools well, but cloud checks are shallow and run daily, not on change.
- **Where we win:** cloud-heavy companies whose auditors ask about configuration drift between audits.
- **Where we lose:** very small companies that only need policy templates for a first SOC 2 Type I.

## Do it yourself

- Open-source scanners plus spreadsheets, owned by one engineer.
- Free to start, but evidence goes stale and nobody owns the questionnaires.
- **Where we win:** after the first audit, when the team realises the spreadsheet has to be rebuilt every year.
- **Where we lose:** teams with a strong platform group that prefers to build.

## Talking points

- Setup in a day, read-only, no agents.
- Evidence is collected on every change, not once a day.
- One price that includes SOC 2 and ISO 27001 on Growth.
