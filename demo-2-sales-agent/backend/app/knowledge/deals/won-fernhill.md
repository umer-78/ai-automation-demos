# Won: Fernhill HR (fictional)

- **Company:** HR software, 340 employees, Azure and AWS.
- **Deal:** Growth plan, $52,000 a year. Closed in 47 days.
- **Why now:** EU expansion. German customers asked for ISO 27001 on top of SOC 2.
- **What worked:** short emails. The champion said our first email was "the only one I read to the end" because it was four sentences.
- **Lesson:** expansion into a new region is a strong trigger. Keep first emails under 100 words.
