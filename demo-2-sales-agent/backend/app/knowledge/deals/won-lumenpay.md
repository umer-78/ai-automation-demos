# Won: Lumenpay (fictional)

- **Company:** fintech, payments API, 220 employees, AWS (14 accounts).
- **Deal:** Growth plan, $48,000 a year. Closed in 34 days.
- **Why now:** an enterprise bank asked for a SOC 2 Type II report before signing. Their new Head of Security had started 6 weeks earlier.
- **What worked:** the first email referenced the bank partnership announced on their blog. We ran a read-only scan in the first call and found 3 public buckets.
- **Lesson:** a new security leader plus a dated compliance deadline is our best pattern. Lead with their news, not our features.
