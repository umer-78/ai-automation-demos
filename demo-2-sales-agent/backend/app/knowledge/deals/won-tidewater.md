# Won: Tidewater Freight Cloud (fictional)

- **Company:** logistics SaaS, 600 employees, 22 AWS accounts, Series C.
- **Deal:** Enterprise, $78,000 a year. Closed in 63 days.
- **Why now:** sales were losing deals in security review; questionnaires took 9 days.
- **What worked:** we offered a sample questionnaire answered from their own (read-only) evidence instead of asking for a meeting.
- **Lesson:** after a funding round, frame the value as faster enterprise deals. A useful offer beats a meeting request.
